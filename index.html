About

Economics taught me to ask why first.

I started in economics because I liked the discipline of understanding why something happened before deciding what to do about it. That instinct followed me into data: first building a performance database for 68,000+ athletes at Reel Analytics, then evaluating technology and risk controls for a streaming platform used by 150 million people at PwC, and now working inside Inspire Brands’ loyalty and personalization programs.

The question I work on has shifted from “what happened” to “what should we build.” That shift is what pulled me toward product management. The parts of the job I like best are the ones that feel like economics: deciding what’s actually worth prioritizing, testing an assumption before spending real resources on it, and turning a business problem into something an engineering team can build.

Outside of that I follow basketball closely enough that I spent two seasons breaking down film for Georgia Tech’s Division I program.
